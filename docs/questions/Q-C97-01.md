# Q-C97-01 — customer bottom-nav middle tab: «فروشگاه‌ها» (v2 board) vs «خرید (به‌زودی)» (v1 boards + UX spec)

**Filed:** 2026-11-27 · **Lane:** C# (C-AND-CUST) · **Stage:** MVP-1 D3 customer channel (C-97 customer boards) · **Blocks:** nothing (implemented per interim rule below)

## The conflict

The customer invoices bottom navigation has a middle tab whose label differs between artifacts:

| Artifact | Middle tab label |
|---|---|
| `docs/design/Customer-Invoices-v2.dc.html` (newest board, page 1) | «فروشگاه‌ها» |
| `docs/design/Customer-Invoices.dc.html`, `Customer-Empty.dc.html`, `Customer-New-Banner.dc.html`, `Customer-Invoice-Detail.dc.html` (v1 boards) | «خرید (به‌زودی، غیرفعال)» |
| `docs/product-ux-spec.md` §6.3 (footer line) | «خرید (به‌زودی)» disabled |

Both variants agree the tab is disabled («به‌زودی», after MVP). They disagree on the WORD.

## What was implemented (interim)

Per Decision 45 (`docs/decisions-made.md`): the newer `Customer-Invoices-v2` board governs shared chrome where artifacts disagree — the tab reads «فروشگاه‌ها» and is disabled. Every state screen keeps its own board's content verbatim. Reversible by a one-line label change once the PM rules.

## Question to the PM / owner

Which label is canonical for the customer bottom-nav middle tab — «فروشگاه‌ها» (v2) or «خرید (به‌زودی)» (v1 + spec)? If «فروشگاه‌ها» wins, `docs/product-ux-spec.md` §6.3 and the v1 boards are stale; if «خرید» wins, `Customer-Invoices-v2` is stale.
